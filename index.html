Material prices

Type money as 1g 20s, 35s, 8c; a bare number is silver. Vendor items are fixed. Unpriced AH mats are outlined and count as zero, which makes every route using them look free, so fill them first. A scan file (data/prices.json) fills these automatically; a price you type stays until a newer scan arrives.

Tailoring crafts at skill

Cost per point = mat cost ÷ skill-up chance. Net = expected disenchant value (or sale price) minus mat cost. Feeds = expected dust/essence per craft; your observed log overrides the seed table once a bracket has enough samples.

Enchanting skill-ups at skill

Each mat is priced at the cheaper of its AH buyout and its effective crafted price (net cost of the cheapest tailoring craft that disenchants into it, divided by expected yield). Source says which won.

Effective mat prices

Plan: skill A → B

Greedy solver: at each point it takes the cheapest craft available. Tailoring runs first and banks its expected disenchant output; Enchanting draws from that bank before buying. The shopping list is what's left to buy; the surplus is what you can sell.

AH shuffle ceiling

Expected disenchant value by item level (what the game rolls on), slot and quality at current prices. Max buyout = EV × ceiling. Greens are usually item level ≈ required level + 4–6; a tooltip addon shows the exact number.

All brackets (greens)

Macro packs

Eight account macros, LP1–LP8, bound once to controller buttons (or F-keys) and never rebound. A session pack fills their bodies. Placeholders in braces are filled from live data: the cheapest enchant per point right now, its gear slot, the shuffle ceilings, the shopping list to the next floor. WoW allows one cast per press; every macro here is one press, one action.

macros-cache.txt (export)

WoW stores account macros in WTF\Account\<acct>\macros-cache.txt. The block below is the LP1–LP8 entries in that file's shape, for the backend to merge while you're logged out. Format from the Classic client; confirm against your own file once before trusting a write.

Observed disenchants

Once a bracket+slot has at least samples, observed probabilities and quantities replace the seed table for it. The Session view has a tap logger; this table is the ledger.

Data

Recipes, the DE yield table (one row per item-level bracket × slot × quality, with per-mat chance and min–max; source is classic, forever or observed) and mats are plain JSON. Edit, then Apply. Export saves everything (characters, prices, log, packs) to the clipboard as JSON; paste into Import to restore. Reset returns to the seed tables.
